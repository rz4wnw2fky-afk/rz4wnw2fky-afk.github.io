French Onion Bucatini

1 hr 40 min4 servingsMainFood & Wine

Ingredients

  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 2 tablespoons neutral oil
  • 4 large red onions, thinly sliced (about 10 cups)
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt, divided
  • ¼ cup dry sherry
  • 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups unsalted beef or chicken broth
  • 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoon fish sauce
  • ¾ teaspoon black pepper
  • 4 thyme sprigs
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 ounces Parmigiano-Reggiano, grated
  • 2 ounces Gruyère, shredded
  • 12 ounces bucatini
  • For the crumbs: 2 tablespoons butter, ¾ cup panko, 2 teaspoons thyme leaves, 1 small grated garlic clove, ½ teaspoon salt, ½ teaspoon pepper, 1 ounce finely grated Gruyère

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter with the oil in a Dutch oven over medium. Add onions and ½ teaspoon salt. Cover and cook until they soften. (10 min)
  2. Uncover and keep cooking, scraping the pan now and then, until the onions are deep brown and jammy. Save ½ cup for the end. (55 min)
  3. Meanwhile, toast the panko in butter with thyme, garlic, salt and pepper until golden. Set aside. (5 min)
  4. Boil the pasta to al dente. Save ½ cup of the cooking water.
  5. Deglaze the onions with sherry until it cooks off. Stir in flour, then broth, Worcestershire, fish sauce, pepper, herbs and the rest of the salt. Simmer until a little thicker, then pull the herbs. (5 min)
  6. Off the heat, stir in both cheeses a handful at a time. Toss in the pasta with splashes of pasta water until glossy. Fold in the saved onions.
  7. Stir the Gruyère into the crumbs and scatter over each bowl. Serve right away.
