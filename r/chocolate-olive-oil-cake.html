Chocolate Olive Oil Cake

1 hr8 to 12 slicesDessertNigella Lawson

Ingredients

  • ⅔ cup olive oil, plus more for the pan
  • 6 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa, sifted
  • ½ cup boiling water
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1½ cups almond meal (or ¾ cup + 1 tablespoon flour)
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3 large eggs

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 325°F. Oil a 9-inch springform pan and line the base.
  2. Whisk the cocoa with the boiling water into a smooth paste. Add the vanilla and let it cool a bit.
  3. Mix the almond meal, baking soda and salt.
  4. Beat the sugar, oil and eggs hard until pale and thick. (3 min)
  5. On low speed, add the cocoa paste, then the dry mix. Pour into the pan and bake until the sides set and the middle is still a bit damp. (45 min)
  6. Cool in the pan, then unmould. Serve warm with ice cream or at room temperature. (10 min)

Gluten free with almond meal, and dairy free.
