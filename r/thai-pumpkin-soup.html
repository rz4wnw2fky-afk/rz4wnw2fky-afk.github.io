Thai Pumpkin Soup

SoupSaved in Notes (ingredients only)

Ingredients

  • 3 pounds pumpkin or winter squash (kabocha is best)
  • 1½ tablespoons coconut oil
  • 3 large shallots, chopped
  • 6 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 2-inch piece ginger, grated
  • 2 stalks lemongrass, minced
  • 12 dried makrut lime leaves (or 6 fresh)
  • 2 tablespoons red curry paste
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon coriander seeds
  • ¼ teaspoon ground turmeric
  • ¼ teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • ½ teaspoon white pepper
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • 3 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 (13.5-ounce) can full-fat coconut milk
  • 1 tablespoon + 1 teaspoon Thai soy sauce
  • 1½ tablespoons coconut sugar
  • To serve: Thai basil, toasted crusty bread, peanut chile crunch
