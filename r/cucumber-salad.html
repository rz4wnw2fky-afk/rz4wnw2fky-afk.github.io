Cucumber Salad with Poppy Seed Buttermilk

10 min4 servingsSaladSaved in Notes

Ingredients

  • ¼ cup buttermilk
  • 3 tablespoons whole-milk plain or Greek yogurt
  • 3 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 1½ tablespoons poppy seeds
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt, plus more
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1½ pounds cucumbers, thinly sliced
  • 4 scallions, thinly sliced

Instructions

  1. Whisk the buttermilk, yogurt, lemon juice, poppy seeds, salt and pepper in a big bowl.
  2. Toss in the cucumbers and scallions. It keeps in the fridge up to 4 hours.

Seed thick slicing cucumbers first so they do not water down the dressing.
