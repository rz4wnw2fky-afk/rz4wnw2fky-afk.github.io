Cottage Cheese Pancakes

25 min4 servingsBreakfastSaved in Notes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (220 g) full-fat cottage cheese
  • 3 large eggs
  • 3 tablespoons milk
  • 2 tablespoons (25 g) sugar
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 cup (128 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • Neutral oil, for the pan
  • Maple syrup or honey, to serve

Instructions

  1. Blend the cottage cheese, eggs, milk, sugar and vanilla until mostly smooth. Fold in the flour and baking soda, leaving some lumps.
  2. Heat a nonstick or cast-iron skillet over medium and wipe it with a little oil. Drop ¼-cup scoops, 2 inches apart.
  3. Cook until bubbly and matte at the edges. (3 min)
  4. Flip and cook until done through. (3 min)
  5. Re-grease and repeat. Serve hot with syrup or honey.

Leftovers keep 3 days. Reheat 30 seconds in the microwave.
