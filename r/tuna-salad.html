Matthew McConaughey's Tuna Salad

10 min + chillMainYahoo Lifestyle

Ingredients

  • ½ cup mayonnaise, plus more to taste
  • 2 teaspoons white wine vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 2 teaspoons wasabi paste
  • 2 teaspoons Italian dressing
  • 3 tablespoons finely chopped red onion
  • 3 tablespoons finely chopped dill pickles
  • ⅓ cup crispy jalapeño chips
  • ¼ cup chopped apple
  • ½ cup fresh corn kernels
  • ½ cup frozen peas
  • 10 ounces tuna in olive oil, drained

Instructions

  1. Whisk the mayo, vinegar, lemon juice, wasabi and Italian dressing.
  2. Stir in the onion, pickles, jalapeño chips, apple, corn and peas.
  3. Fold in the tuna gently. Add more mayo if it looks dry.
  4. Chill a few hours or overnight. Serve with extra pickles.
