Miso Brown Butter Fried Rice

15 min2 to 4 servingsMainNYT Cooking

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons white miso
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce, plus more to taste
  • 1 teaspoon honey, plus more to taste
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 4 cups cold cooked long-grain rice (day-old is best)
  • 4 eggs, beaten and lightly salted
  • 4 scallions, thinly sliced

Instructions

  1. Set the miso, soy sauce and honey together in a small bowl. No need to stir.
  2. Melt the butter in a large pan over medium heat. Swirl until it foams, smells nutty and shows brown specks. (4 min)
  3. Pull the pan off the heat. Stir in the miso mix fast until it turns into a thick sauce.
  4. Back on the heat, add the cold rice. Break up clumps and toss until every grain is coated. (3 min)
  5. Push the rice aside. Scramble the eggs in the open space into soft curds, then fold them in.
  6. Taste. Add a splash of soy or a drizzle of honey if it needs it. Fold in the scallions and serve right away.
