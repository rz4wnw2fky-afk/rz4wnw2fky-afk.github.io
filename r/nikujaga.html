Japanese Beef and Potato Stew (Nikujaga)

55 minServes 3 to 4MainJosh's recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 lb thinly sliced beef chuck or ribeye (sukiyaki-style thin slices work best, cut into bite-sized pieces)
  • 3 medium Yukon Gold or Russet potatoes, peeled and cut into large chunks (about 2-inch pieces)
  • 2 medium yellow onions, cut into wedges
  • 1 large carrot, peeled and cut into oblique chunks (rangiri)
  • 1 cup green beans, trimmed (optional, added at the end for color and crunch)
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil or vegetable oil
  • Braising broth: 1 cup dashi stock (or water mixed with Japanese dashi powder)
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp mirin
  • 2 tbsp sake
  • 1.5 tbsp sugar

Instructions

  1. Sear the beef: Preheat your Musui-Kamado pot using [MED] heat. Add the oil. Add the thinly sliced beef and stir until it just changes color (it doesn't need to be fully cooked through yet).
  2. Add the onion wedges, carrot chunks, and potato chunks to the pot, tossing everything gently for about 2 minutes to let the vegetables absorb a bit of the savory beef fat. (2 min)
  3. Pour in the broth: Pour the dashi stock, soy sauce, mirin, sake, and sugar directly over the beef and vegetables.
  4. Braise in the Kamado: Secure the lid. Set your Kamado to [MED/LOW] and simmer for about 25 to 30 minutes. (The precision seal keeps the liquid circulating so the potatoes cook evenly all the way through without boiling dry.) (30 min)
  5. Add finishing touch: Open the lid, drop in the green beans, and let them simmer uncovered for the last 3 to 5 minutes until tender-crisp and bright green. (5 min)
  6. Serve: Ladle into warm bowls, making sure every portion gets a great mix of tender beef, melt-in-your-mouth potatoes, and sweet carrots, all coated in that glossy, savory broth.

Prep 15 minutes. Cook about 35 to 40 minutes.
