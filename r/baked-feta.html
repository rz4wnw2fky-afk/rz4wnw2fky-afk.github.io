Baked Feta with Honey

30 min4 to 6 servingsSnackNYT Cooking

Ingredients

  • 1 (8-ounce) block feta, blotted dry
  • 1 sprig thyme (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • Black pepper
  • Toasted pita wedges, to serve
  • Tomatoes, roasted beets, nuts or pickles (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Set the feta in a small baking dish, add thyme and cover with oil. Bake until soft and springy, not melted. (8 min)
  2. Turn on the broiler. Warm the honey until it brushes easily and paint it over the feta.
  3. Broil until the top browns and bubbles. Add pepper and serve hot with pita.
