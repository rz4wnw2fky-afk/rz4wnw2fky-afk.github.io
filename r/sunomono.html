Japanese Cucumber Salad (Sunomono)

1 hr 20 min4 servingsSaladMildly Meandering

Ingredients

  • 2 to 3 cucumbers, thinly sliced
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ¼ cup rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon mirin
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 2 teaspoons soy sauce
  • Sesame seeds, to garnish

Instructions

  1. Toss the cucumbers with the salt, cover and let them sit. (10 min)
  2. Stir the vinegar, mirin, sugar and soy sauce together.
  3. Squeeze the water out of the cucumbers and move them to a clean bowl.
  4. Pour on the dressing, cover and let it rest before serving. (1 hr)
  5. Top with sesame seeds.
