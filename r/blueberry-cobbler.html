Chez Panisse's Blueberry Cobbler

1 hr 15 min4 to 6 servingsDessertNYT Cooking

Ingredients

  • 4½ cups fresh blueberries
  • ⅓ cup sugar
  • 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
  • For the dough: 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1½ tablespoons sugar
  • 2¼ teaspoons baking powder
  • 6 tablespoons cold unsalted butter, in ½-inch pieces
  • ¾ cup heavy cream, plus more to serve

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Toss the berries with the sugar and flour.
  2. Mix the flour, salt, sugar and baking powder. Cut in the butter until it looks like coarse meal. Stir in the cream just until damp.
  3. Put the berries in a 1½-quart dish. Shape the dough into ½-inch-thick patties and lay them on top. Bake until brown and bubbling. (40 min)
  4. Cool a little. Serve warm with cream.
