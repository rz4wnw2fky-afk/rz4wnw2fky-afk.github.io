White Bean Gratin

45 min5 to 6 servingsSideSaved in Notes

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • ¾ cup heavy cream
  • ¼ cup fresh thyme leaves
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 2 teaspoons black pepper
  • 4 (15-ounce) cans cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cups grated Gruyère (8 ounces)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 325°F. Put one rack in the middle and one 6 inches under the broiler.
  2. Stir the melted butter with the cream, thyme, salt and pepper.
  3. In a 10-inch skillet, layer half the beans, ½ cup cheese, then the rest of the beans. Pour the cream over, give it a shake, and top with the remaining cheese.
  4. Cover with foil and bake on the middle rack. (30 min)
  5. Uncover and broil until the cheese browns and bubbles. Rest before serving. (3 min)

Swaps from your note: Great Northern, navy or pinto beans work. Smoked Gouda or Parmesan can stand in for Gruyère.
