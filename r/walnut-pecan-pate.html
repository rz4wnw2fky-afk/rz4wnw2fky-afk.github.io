Walnut Pecan Pâté

20 minSnackOutpost Natural Foods

Ingredients

  • 1¾ cups walnuts
  • ¾ cup pecans
  • ¼ medium red onion
  • 2 large garlic cloves
  • 1½ pounds plain firm tofu
  • 2 tablespoons cider vinegar
  • ½ cup tamari
  • ½ teaspoon cayenne
  • ½ cup finely chopped parsley

Instructions

  1. Toast the walnuts and pecans at 350°F, or in a dry pan. (10 min)
  2. Roughly chop the onion and garlic.
  3. Blend everything except the parsley in a food processor until smooth.
  4. Stir in the parsley. Chill until you need it.
