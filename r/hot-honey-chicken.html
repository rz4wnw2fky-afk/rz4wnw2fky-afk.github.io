Roasted Chicken Thighs with Hot Honey and Lime

40 min4 servingsMainSaved in Notes

Ingredients

  • 3 pounds bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • Salt and black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon onion powder
  • ½ teaspoon garlic powder
  • 2 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 2 tablespoons hot sauce
  • 1 lime
  • 2 tablespoons mild honey

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 450°F. Season the chicken on a foil-lined sheet pan with salt, pepper, onion and garlic powder. Roast skin side up until browned. (20 min)
  2. Whisk the melted butter and hot sauce. Set half aside. Brush the rest over the chicken and roast until golden and cooked through. (15 min)
  3. Zest the lime and halve it. Whisk the honey and 1 teaspoon zest into the reserved butter mix. Brush over the chicken, squeeze on the lime and serve.

Your note says: serve it with the Cucumber Salad. Tabasco runs hot; Louisiana-style sauce is milder.
